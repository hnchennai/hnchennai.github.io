Learn chess one clear step at a time

Chess Coach is a free Android app. Rani, your coach, starts from your level, shows you one next step, and explains every move in plain words.

Download for Android See the source code

Coming to Google Play. Until then, the download is the installable app file from our releases page.

What you get

  • A short level check, so lessons start where you are: Beginner, Improver or Club.
  • Lessons, puzzles and practice games that build on each other.
  • Plain words for beginners. No ratings, notation or engine talk until you want them.
  • Unlimited hints, and a review after each game that shows what to try next time.
  • Works offline. The chess engine runs on your phone.

Your data

Your progress, games and settings are saved on your phone. The app has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or contacts.

Free software

Chess Coach is free software under the GNU GPL version 3. The full source code is at github.com/hnchennai/chess-coach.

The app includes the Stockfish chess engine (GPL version 3) and chess.js. The 3D pieces are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.
