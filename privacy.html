Privacy policy

Applies to the Chess Coach app for Android (package com.suresh.chesscoach). Last updated: 21 November 2026.

The short version

  • Your games, progress and settings stay on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • The app has no sign-up. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. The ads are not personalised. Google collects some device data to show and measure them.
  • The app has no analytics and no tracking of its own.

Who we are

Chess Coach is published by Chess Coach ("we"). Contact: handnchennai@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these things in its own storage on your device:

  • Your level, lesson and puzzle progress.
  • Your recent games (up to 30) and their reviews.
  • Your settings, such as the coach and piece style.

We do not have a server that receives this data. The chess engine runs on your phone, so your moves are not sent anywhere.

Ads

The app shows a banner ad using Google AdMob. There are no ads on your first day, during a lesson, game, puzzle or review, or over the board. The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only, which means ads are not chosen from your past activity or interests. The highest ad content rating is PG.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

DataExamples
Device identifiersThe advertising ID of your device
Device and network informationIP address (which shows an approximate location), device model, operating system, language
Ad activityWhich ads were shown and whether they were tapped
DiagnosticsCrash and performance data from the ads component

Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent. In Europe and the UK the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings, under Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, under Privacy, then Ads.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send your games or moves to any AI service.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking of our own.

Children

Chess Coach is meant for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has reached us, write to us and we will help.

Deleting your data

Everything the app saves is on your phone. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. For data held by Google for ads, use the choices above and your Google account settings.

Open source

The app is free software under the GNU GPL version 3. You can read exactly what it does in the source code.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
