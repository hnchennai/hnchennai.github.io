Privacy policy

Applies to the Chess Coach app for Android (package com.suresh.chesscoach). Last updated: 19 November 2026.

The short version

  • Your games, progress and settings stay on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • The app has no sign-up. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Chess Coach is published by Chess Coach ("we"). Contact: handnchennai@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app saves these things in its own storage on your device:

  • Your level, lesson and puzzle progress.
  • Your recent games (up to 30) and their reviews.
  • Your settings, such as the coach and piece style.

We do not have a server that receives this data. The chess engine runs on your phone, so your moves are not sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or sell your data.
  • We do not send your games or moves to any AI service.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking.

Children

Chess Coach is meant for players aged 13 and older. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the app saves is on your phone. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Open source

The app is free software under the GNU GPL version 3. You can read exactly what it does in the source code.

Changes

A later version of the app may show ads. Before that version is released, we will update this page to say what the ads service collects and what choices you have, and we will change the date at the top.
